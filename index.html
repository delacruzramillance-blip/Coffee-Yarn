OUR STORY

Coffee-Yarn is a cozy coffee shop where people can enjoy their favorite coffee while having good conversations. The name combines coffee and “yarn,” representing the simple conversations and stories shared over a cup of coffee.

MISSION

Our mission is to provide quality coffee and a comfortable place where customers can relax, connect, and enjoy meaningful conversations with friends and loved ones.
